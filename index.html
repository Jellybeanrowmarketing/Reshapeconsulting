The Reshape Process

Ten steps, zero guesswork.

From first contact to final warranty — structured so you always know exactly what comes next.

Ten steps, organized into four phases — tap any phase to see exactly what happens inside it.

01

Connect

Getting to know your project, your goals, and each other.

Steps 01–03
01

Initial Contact

Reach out by phone, email, text, social media, or our website. Our team responds promptly and begins gathering what's needed to understand your goals.

02

Initial Connection

We ask for photos of the space, a brief overview of your goals, and your availability for a discovery call — so our first meeting is focused on you.

03

Discovery Call

We discuss goals, investment range, timeline, priorities, and expectations, introduce the Reshape process, and determine whether we're the right fit.

02

Plan

Turning your vision into a detailed, buildable plan.

Steps 04–06
04

Proposal

We outline scope of work, project investment, and next steps — clarity and transparency so you decide with confidence.

05

Onboarding & Site Visit

Once approved, we visit to verify measurements, assess conditions, and begin onboarding — gathering everything needed to move into design.

06

Design & Planning

We develop concepts, finalise selections, and prepare construction documentation — coordinating schedules, procurement, budgets, and logistics behind the scenes.

03

Build

Where the work happens — tracked, coordinated, and visible.

Steps 07–08
07

Pre-Construction

We prepare the site, conduct walkthroughs, and establish communication systems. A Reshape Job Board is installed as the central hub for schedules and quality control.

08

Renovation

Our team and trusted trade partners work through a structured system. Every task and milestone is tracked — and you have access to the same project systems we use.

04

Complete

A careful handover, and a relationship that continues.

Steps 09–10
09

Walkthrough & Review

As construction nears completion, we walk through together. Any outstanding items are documented, addressed, and completed before final handover.

10

Close-Out & Warranty

A final clean, aftercare, warranty review, and permanent access to your project file. We remain available long after you've begun enjoying your new space.

Investment, without the pressure

Finding your comfort zone — not your limit.

We're not in the business of pushing budgets. During your discovery call and proposal, we map your priorities against your investment range and shape the plan until it sits confidently in the middle — not stretched thin at the edge of what you're willing to spend.

If a scope drifts toward the edge of your comfort zone, we say so early and work through the options together — so nothing about your investment is a surprise on the day you sign.

Understanding the investment.

Every project is unique, but most renovations fall into a few clear tiers. These ranges help you understand scope and budget before our complimentary discovery call — where we tailor a detailed proposal to your exact space.

Tier 1 · Reduced-Scale
Essential Bathroom Refresh
Ideal for refreshing an existing primary bathroom while maintaining the current layout.
$15K–25K
Typical investment
  • Existing layout remains unchanged
  • Vanity replacement
  • Flooring replacement
  • Updated shower or tub surround
  • New plumbing fixtures
  • Updated lighting fixtures
  • Cosmetic wall & ceiling improvements
  • No major plumbing or electrical modifications

Elevated Comfort

$24K–40K
Typical investment
  • Full renovation within existing footprint
  • Heated tile flooring

  • Quartz surfaces
  • Custom shower enclosure
  • Minor layout refinements
  • Enhanced lighting design
  • Decorative wall treatments
